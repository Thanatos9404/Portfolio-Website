06 / About

Building between classes.

I'm a BCA student at BIT Mesra's Jaipur campus with a minor in Artificial Intelligence. Most of my learning happens between coursework and whatever I'm shipping that week.

LLMSlim started with a question I kept running into: why are we sending so much context to models? Krishyak took the same engineering into fields and mandis. I like the whole path from research prototype to product: framing the problem, testing the model, building the interface and putting it in front of real people.

Today I split my time between LLMSlim, client work through AMEYOR, and fixing things upstream in the open-source tools I depend on. If you need a Python backend, a retrieval or agent system, an AI feature that actually works, or a fast, polished website, let's talk.
